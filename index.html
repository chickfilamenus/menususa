    28 items on the regular menu cost $5 or less before tax. They include the Chicken Biscuit (about $4.95), every side, the cookies, brownies and Icedream treats, and most drinks. If you are watching your spending, a Chicken Biscuit in the morning, or a medium Waffle Potato Fries with an Icedream Cup (about $4.70), keeps you at $5 or less before tax.

    Salad prices

    Salads are the priciest part of the regular menu. The Spicy Southwest Salad is about $10.79, and the Cobb Salad and Market Salad about $11.25 each. They are also the most complete meals on their own, with chicken, toppings and dressing included, so many people skip the side. Dressings, toppings and the grilled or fried chicken choices are covered on the Chick-fil-A Salads Menu page.

    How these prices are collected

    The prices on this page are typical U.S. averages taken from published menu price surveys and last checked in November 2026. They are not taken from one restaurant. Chick-fil-A restaurants are run by local owner-operators, so the price in your town can be higher or lower. Airport, mall, stadium and campus locations, and delivery apps, are usually at the top of the range. Always check the official app for your exact restaurant before you order.

    Simple ways to spend less in 2026

    • Pick up instead of delivery. Delivery apps usually charge more per item and add fees.
    • Go bigger on nuggets. The 12-count costs less per nugget than the 8-count.
    • Choose a cheaper drink. Milk and tea cost less than frosted drinks and milkshakes.
    • Collect rewards. Chick-fil-A One members earn points they can spend on free items.
    • Plan with the slider. Set your budget before you get in line.

    Chick-fil-A price questions

    How much does Chick-fil-A cost in 2026?

    In 2026 most Chick-fil-A entrees cost between about $5.95 and $9.95, sides about $2.65 to $4.65, and drinks about $1.95 to $5.25. A typical meal with a side and a drink is around $11 to $13 before tax.

    What can I get at Chick-fil-A for $10?

    With $10 including about 7.5% sales tax, you can get a 12-count of Nuggets, or a Chicken Sandwich with a medium Waffle Potato Fries (about $8.94 before tax). A full meal with a drink needs about $11.34 or more with tax. Use the budget slider on this page to try any amount.

    What is the cheapest meal at Chick-fil-A?

    The cheapest full meal is usually the 8-count Nuggets with a Kale Crunch Side and a 1% Milk or Chocolate Milk, at about $10.55 before tax. For a single item, the Icedream Cup at about $1.75 is the cheapest.

    How much are Chick-fil-A salads in 2026?

    The Spicy Southwest Salad costs about $10.79, and the Cobb Salad and Market Salad about $11.25 each.

    Are Chick-fil-A menu prices the same everywhere?

    No. Each restaurant is run by a local operator who sets prices for that area. Airports, malls, campuses and delivery apps are usually more expensive than a regular restaurant.

    How often do Chick-fil-A prices change?

    Restaurants can change prices at any time. This page shows the date the prices were last checked, so compare with the official app before you order.
